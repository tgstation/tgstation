BODYPARTS_DEFAULT_MAXIMUMThe standard amount of bodyparts a carbon has. Currently 6, HEAD/L_ARM/R_ARM/CHEST/L_LEG/R_LEG
LIMB_MAX_HP_PROSTHESISLimb Health The max damage a limb can take before it stops taking damage. Used by the max_damage var.
LIMB_MAX_HP_ALIEN_LARVAXenomorph Limbs
LIMB_BODY_DAMAGE_COEFFICIENT_ADVANCEDLimb Body Damage Coefficient A multiplication of the burn and brute damage that the limb's stored damage contributes to its attached mob's overall wellbeing. For instance, if a limb has 50 damage, and has a coefficient of 50%, the human is considered to have suffered 25 damage to their total health.

BIO_INORGANICHas absolutely fucking nothing, no wounds
BIO_BONEHas bone - allows the victim to suffer T2-T3 bone blunt wounds
BIO_FLESHHas flesh - allows the victim to suffer fleshy slash pierce and burn wounds
BIO_METALHas metal - allows the victim to suffer robotic blunt and burn wounds
BIO_WOODHas wood - should probably be able to catch on fire, or something
BIO_WIREDIs wired internally - allows the victim to suffer electrical wounds (robotic T1-T3 slash/pierce), relevant wounds currently unimplemented
BIO_BLOODEDHas bloodflow - can suffer bleeding wounds and can bleed
BIO_JOINTEDIs connected by a joint - can suffer T1 bone blunt wounds (dislocation) and T1 robotic blunt (loosened screws) if METAL as well
BIO_CHITINSkin is covered in thick chitin and is resistant to cutting
BIO_STONEthe bodypart is made of rocks.
BIO_JELLYLimb is comprised entirely of slime jelly - can be cannibalized by any jelly person with the ability to eat their own limbs to replenish their own slime (blood)
BIO_ROBOTICRobotic - can suffer all metal/wired wounds, such as: T1-3 burn/brute, except loosened screws. slash/pierce wounds currently unimplemented.
BIO_FLESH_BONEHas flesh and bone - See BIO_BONE and BIO_FLESH
BIO_STANDARD_UNJOINTEDStandard humanoid - can bleed and suffer all flesh/bone wounds, such as: T1-3 slash/pierce/burn/blunt, except dislocations. Think human heads/chests
BIO_STANDARD_JOINTEDStandard humanoid limbs - can bleed and suffer all flesh/bone wounds, such as: T1-3 slash/pierce/burn/blunt. Can also bleed, and be dislocated. Think human arms and legs
BIO_STANDARD_ALIENXenomorph limbs (xenos are immune to wounds anyhow)
BIOSTATE_HAS_SKINBiological state that has some kind of skin that can be cut.
LIMB_HAS_SKINChecks if a bodypart lacks both flesh and metal, meaning it has no skin to cut.
BIOSTATE_HAS_BONESBiological state that has some kind of bones that can be sawed.
LIMB_HAS_BONESChecks if a bodypart lacks both bone and metal, meaning it has no bones to saw.
BIOSTATE_HAS_VESSELSBiological state that has some kind of vessels that can be clamped.
LIMB_HAS_VESSELSChecks if a bodypart lacks both blood and wires, meaning it has no vessels to manipulate.
HUSKED_BURNHusked from burns
HUSKED_ZOMBIEHusked from zombification
HUSK_OVERLAY_NONEDo not draw this bodypart overlay on husks
HUSK_OVERLAY_GRAYSCALEDraw this overlay on husks but grayscale it
HUSK_OVERLAY_NORMALDraw this overlay on husks as normal
LIMB_ITEM_GAUZEGauze slot, asserted to be wrap items
LIMB_ITEM_TOURNIQUETTourniquet slot
GET_BODYPART_COEFFICIENTFor scaling the effectiveness of certain effects to the total bodypart count
