cooldown
/datum/bt_node/decorator/cooldown

Checks if a blackboard cooldown key is off cooldown before ticking the child, then sets it when the child finishes replacing the key_off_cooldown

cooldown_duration is the duration of the cooldown (or lower bound if max is also set) cooldown_duration_max is the upper bounds lock_on_succeed = TRUE (default): lock after child SUCCESS only. lock_on_succeed = FALSE: lock after any completion (SUCCESS or FAILURE).
